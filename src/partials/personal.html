Anime and mediaAnime y medios

He has publicly identified as an otaku, while distancing himself from some of the archetype's more visible practices: by his own account he has attended only one convention, and has worn cosplay only a handful of times. He estimates having watched around one thousand anime series in total, and reports continuing the story in print for approximately fifty of those through manga.4

Se identifica públicamente como otaku, aunque toma distancia de algunas prácticas más visibles del arquetipo: por declaración propia ha asistido a una sola convención y se ha disfrazado en cosplay sólo unas pocas veces. Estima haber visto cerca de mil animes en total y haber continuado la historia en formato impreso para unos cincuenta de ellos a través de mangas.4
