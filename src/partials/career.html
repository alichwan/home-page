Between August 2020 and December 2020, Pavel was a Junior Data Scientist at CORFO — the Chilean economic development agency, to which thousands of innovation projects apply each year for competitive grants. Working alongside Martín Benimeli, he designed a cross‑contest similarity metric for grant applications, with the goal of flagging potential duplicate proposals, including projects previously funded in prior years. The work made heavy use of natural‑language processing, as the free‑text fields of the applications carried most of the relevant information.41

From January to July 2022 he returned as a Machine Learning Intern, this time building a text‑based attribute classifier using scikit‑learn and TensorFlow, in order to predict missing categorical fields that applicants were not required to complete. The classifier expanded the available labelled data through automated multi‑dimensional classification, improving overall model training coverage.1
