Early lifePrimeros años

Pavel was born on 25 February 1997 in Santiago, Chile, and grew up in the same city. He is the eldest of three siblings.4 He has stated in autobiographical writing that, as the firstborn, he experienced a degree of self‑imposed pressure not to disappoint his younger brothers, and that being the child of divorced parents required him to take on roles that were sometimes uncomfortable.4

He has lived his entire life in Chile and, as of 2026, has not travelled outside the country.4 He describes himself as preferring winter and autumn to the warmer seasons, and lists cats and foxes as his favourite animals.4
