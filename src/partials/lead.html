Alex Pavel Medina Jorquera (born 25 February 1997), commonly known as Pavel, is a Chilean data scientist and software engineer based in Santiago. He holds degrees in Mathematical and Computational Engineering and a Master's in Computer Science with a specialisation in machine learning from the Pontifical Catholic University of Chile.2

Between 2020 and 2022 he worked for the Chilean economic development agency CORFO on similarity metrics and natural‑language classifiers used to triage thousands of public innovation grant applications.5 Since April 2023 he has been a Service Reliability Engineer at Zippedi, where he maintains operational reliability across a fleet of around 200 autonomous retail‑inventory robots deployed in chains including Jumbo, Sodimac, Unimarc and Kmart.3

His master's research, supervised by Jorge Baier and Rodrigo Toro Icarte, evaluated MILP, SAT and Answer Set Programming as alternative formal methods for the synthesis of minimal deterministic finite automata from labelled example traces.2 Outside of work he is a self‑described otaku who has watched approximately one thousand anime series, and maintains curated music playlists which together exceed twenty‑four hours of listening time.4
